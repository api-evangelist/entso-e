On 12 August, a solar eclipse will temporarily reduce solar power generation across large parts of Europe as the Moon passes in front of the Sun. Under clear-sky conditions, photovoltaic (PV) output could decrease by up to 9.7 GW across Europe, creating a rapid but predictable change in generation patterns between 19:15-21:30 CEST. Thanks to well-established coordination mechanisms and proactive preparations, ENTSO-E and Europe’s Transmission System Operators (TSOs) are ready to ensure the secure and stable operation of the electricity system throughout the event.

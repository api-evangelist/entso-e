The Expert Panel established to investigate the blackout that occurred on 18 May 2025 in North Macedonia is publishing its final report today. The report identifies the causes of the blackout and outlines recommendations to strengthen the resilience of Europe’s interconnected electricity system. It was prepared by an 18-member Panel comprising representatives from Transmission System Operators (TSOs), ACER, and National Regulatory Authorities (NRAs).

The ITC mechanism functions according to the terms defined in the ITC Agreement, compensating transmission system operators for costs associated with hosting transit flows (i.e. facilitating the transfer of power between two countries). This mechanism aims to incentivize the hosting of cross border flows and to facilitate the creation of an effectively competitive pan-European electricity market.

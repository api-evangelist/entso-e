As Europe works to make electricity the most attractive energy choice, ENTSO-E’s latest overview of transmission tariffs 2024 and 2025 provides valuable transparency on how transmission costs are recovered across Europe. Because future-proof electricity bills and future-proof grids must go hand in hand.

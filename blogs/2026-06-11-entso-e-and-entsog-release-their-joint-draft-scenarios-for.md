(Brussels, 10 June, PR0369-26) ENTSO-E and ENTSOG released today their draft joint Scenarios, forming the basis for electricity, hydrogen and gas Ten-Year Network Development Plans (TYNDPs) 2026. The draft Scenarios highlight a structural transformation of Europe’s energy landscape towards climate neutrality by 2050, with overall demand declining due to efficiency gains and electrification.

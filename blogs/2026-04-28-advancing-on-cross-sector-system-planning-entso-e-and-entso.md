ENTSO-E and ENTSOG, with the involvement of ENNOH, have submitted the Interlinked Modelling Report to the European Commission. The report guides coordinated cross-sector infrastructure planning, and outlines the interactions between the electricity, gas and hydrogen sectors within the respective Ten-Year Network Development Plans (TYNDPs). The report also outlines how cross-sector planning will be progressively embedded in the TYNDPs.

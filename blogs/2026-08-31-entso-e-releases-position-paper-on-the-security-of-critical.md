Europe’s transmission grid and its secure operation remain essential for powering our society, and TSOs are responsible for ensuring reliable electricity supply for all Europeans at all times. As the energy system becomes increasingly interconnected, digitalised and electrified, safeguarding critical infrastructure against evolving physical and cyber threats is more important than ever. Today, ENTSO-E provides recommendations to strengthen the EU framework for protecting our electricity infrastructure.

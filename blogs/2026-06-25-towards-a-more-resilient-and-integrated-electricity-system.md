Brussels, 25 June – ENTSO-E and DSO Entity announce the launch of their new joint website, the TSO-DSO Cooperation Platform, a dedicated digital hub bringing together all major initiatives, projects, and resources developed through Transmission System Operator (TSO) and Distribution System Operator (DSO) cooperation. As the energy transition accelerates, closer TSO-DSO collaboration will be essential to support renewable integration, digitalisation, security of supply and a more integrated electricity market.

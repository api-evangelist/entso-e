Brussels, 29 May 2026 – ENTSO-E publishes its assessment of Europe’s resource adequacy for the summer of 2026. The report, which assesses generation, demand, and other flexible resources across the pan-European system, confirms no systemic adequacy risks for most parts of Europe, while highlighting residual concerns in some isolated or weakly interconnected regions. The Summer Outlook report is designed to inform national and European authorities on system risks.
